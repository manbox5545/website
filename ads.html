05 / FAIR QUESTIONS

Good to know
before go-time.

Clear expectations make better campaigns.

Can I choose my own budget?

Yes. Share a budget range in the brief. Mpoxel Ads will need to confirm whether the requested campaign and placement are feasible within it.

Can I pick the campaign duration?

You can request preferred start and end dates. The final timeline depends on review, availability, payment and the written campaign agreement.

Are impressions or clicks guaranteed?

Not by default. The campaign proposal must specify whether the deliverable is a fixed-term placement, estimated reach or a defined quantity of impressions. Estimates are not guarantees unless explicitly agreed in writing.

What ads are allowed?

Ads are reviewed before launch. Scams, malware, deceptive claims and prohibited products are not accepted. Incentivised traffic or rewarded ad views are only used where the relevant advertising provider expressly permits them.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions are provided only after the campaign scope and terms are confirmed. This website does not collect payment details.
